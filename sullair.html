Sullair Logistics Platform

We turned a spreadsheet-heavy operation into a single, traceable workflow that connects internal teams with carriers. The focus was clarity, speed, and fewer manual handoffs across logistics and administration.

Context

Orders lived across emails and disconnected files, which delayed approvals and made billing corrections too common. The core challenge was to unify complex flows and enable real-time collaboration with transport partners.

Goals

  • Reduce rework by ~40% with cleaner request data.
  • Shorten order turnaround time by ~30%.
  • Lower support tickets tied to manual entry.

Process

Four weeks of workshops and discovery with Purchasing, IT, Tax, Billing, and Sales. We mapped workflows, shaped the information architecture, and prioritized an MVP focused on measurable impact.

Outcome

A single platform that ties logistics, admin, and carriers into one flow—improving visibility, speeding decisions, and setting the foundation for future modules.
